## Fixed

- Sticky canary routing's threshold could truncate to 0 under integer division whenever a canary's weight share fell below 1/10000 of its group's total weight, permanently starving that canary of every sticky-routed key with no error or log.

## Security

- The openai and openaicompat streaming decoders silently swallowed a mid-stream `{"error":...}` failure frame (unmarshaled into a zero-valued chunk with no error), letting a real mid-generation failure be billed and cached as a normal, truncated-but-successful completion. Both decoders now surface a real error on that frame, matching every sibling adapter's existing mid-stream failure detection.
